Set up and manage your OpenScoreboard!

OpenScoreboard is a 64×32 LED scoreboard that follows the games you care about. This app gets it on Wi-Fi, pairs it with your phone, and decides what it shows.

- Set favorite teams and leagues to watch
- Manually select games and events
- Configure off-modes so your Scoreboard is always showing something interesting

It covers pro and college football, basketball, hockey and baseball, soccer, golf and racing. When nothing is on, the board can show a clock or the weather instead.
